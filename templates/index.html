Streamlined Workflow

How Campus Reunification Works

A transparent 3-step process to securely recover lost belongings without public exposure

01

1. Report with Vault

File a lost or found report. Put public descriptions on the listing, and lock secret identifying marks (serial numbers, hidden scratches) in your confidential vault.

02

2. Local AI Matching

Our local TF-IDF text similarity engine scans descriptions, categories, dates, and locations to suggest high-probability potential matches with honest reasons.

03

3. Verify & Handover

Claimants submit ownership proof. The finder verifies the claimant's explanation against the vault details, approves the claim, and finalizes the handover!
